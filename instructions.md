Some things to consider when approaching the task:

- Stick to the time limit - the task is about prioritisation. There may not be time to do everything!;

- Keep note of the decisions you made and the why behind them (ie the trade offs) as this will form the discussion follow up interview;

- Be pragmatic with tooling - you may use some AI coding assistant or some libraries/frameworks but you should be able to clearly explain what the code is doing and why and avoid overcomplicated code that is not explainable - you should be able to clearly explain all the decisions and trade offs in your solution!
